# Two ECG-free aids: guessing the timing offset, and a per-beat confidence (B6, C8)

## B6: can the SCG alone predict a person's timing offset?

Numbers: `results/delay_features/summary.md` (generated by `scripts/delay_features.py`).

Much of the error for a new person is a constant shift (`docs/04_gans.md`), and a shift measured with a
little ECG fixes part of it (`docs/05_calibration.md`). Could features of the SCG alone guess it?

The SCG features:
- heart rate;
- the width of the main vibration peak;
- the lag and height of the second peak;
- the dominant frequency.

They are fed to a ridge regression, leave one person out, and judged on mean absolute error of the
guessed shift.

| dataset | people | no shift | training people's mean shift | SCG features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CEBS (same sensor as training) | 16 | **7.7 ms** | 8.0 ms | 13.4 ms |
| SensSmartTech (new sensor) | 32 | 35.0 ms | 26.3 ms | **21.2 ms** |

- **On the training sensor, no.** The shifts are small (about 8 ms), and the features make the guess worse.
- **On a new sensor, partly.** The shifts there are large and partly systematic: the new sensor sits
  elsewhere on the chest, so applying the average offset alone already helps. The SCG's dominant
  frequency adds a little more (Spearman -0.48 with the shift). 21 ms is still about a quarter of a QRS width
  off, while a few seconds of ECG gets the timing to within 2-3 ms.
- So an ECG-free timing fix is possible only in part, and only when moving to a new sensor.

## C8: does seed disagreement flag bad beats?

Numbers: `results/confidence/summary.md` (generated by `scripts/confidence.py`).

The 3 U-Net seeds of each fold are run on every beat of the 16 unseen development people (22,038
beats). Confidence is how much the seeds agree on the beat; quality is how well their mean matches the
real beat.

- **It is a weak signal.** Spearman between confidence and quality is 0.36 over all beats. Within a person
  it ranges from -0.66 to +0.79.
- **Hiding uncertain beats helps only modestly:**

  | least confident beats hidden | median quality of the rest | rest with quality < 0.5 |
  |---|---|---|
  | none | 0.78 | 26% |
  | 10% | 0.81 | 21% |
  | 30% | 0.84 | 17% |

- **It cannot see errors the seeds share.** For subject 6, whose output is shifted in time (`docs/04_gans.md`),
  all three seeds make the same mistake: median seed agreement 0.90, median beat quality -0.05. Seed
  disagreement catches random failures, not systematic ones, and for a new person the systematic
  failures (timing, beat shape) are the big ones.
- So use it as a secondary display hint at most. The fix for systematic errors is calibration, which
  removes them (`docs/05_calibration.md`, `docs/12_cheap_calibration.md`).
