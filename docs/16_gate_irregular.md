# A heartbeat check for irregular rhythms (C7)

Numbers: `results/gate2/summary.md` (generated by `scripts/gate2.py`). No models are involved. The set-up
is the same as `docs/10_gate.md`: 8 s windows, thresholds from each fold's 2 tune people, and the same
negatives.

The periodicity check of `docs/10_gate.md` passes only 46% of real windows that contain an irregular
beat, and 64% of all windows for subject 18, the most irregular person. Two scores that do not assume a
fixed beat interval:

- **Consistency:** the median correlation of each detected vibration beat with the window's median beat.
  Do the beats look alike, whenever they come?
- **Regularity:** the share of beat-to-beat intervals within 20% of the median interval. An early beat
  disturbs two intervals; scrambled fragments land at random gaps.

## Fraction of windows that pass

| input | periodicity | consistency | periodicity OR (consistency AND regularity) |
|---|---|---|---|
| real SCG, all windows | 0.92 | 0.93 | **0.98** |
| real windows containing an irregular beat | 0.46 | 0.99 | **0.97** |
| subject 18 (6% irregular beats) | 0.64 | 1.00 | **1.00** |
| white noise | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| pink noise | 0.00 | 0.06 | 0.06 |
| brown noise | 0.00 | 0.34 | 0.31 |
| SCG shuffled in 50 ms blocks | 0.00 | 0.45 | 0.36 |
| SCG shuffled in 200 ms blocks | 0.00 | 0.74 | 0.51 |

## What it shows

- **No single check here is both safe and fair to irregular hearts.** Periodicity rejects scrambled and
  slowly drifting signals almost perfectly but fails half the windows with an irregular beat. The beat
  scores keep irregular rhythms (97-100%) but let through a third of brown noise and half of the 200 ms
  scrambled SCG. Those still contain whole vibration beats, so in a sense they are partly real.
- **The practical answer is two levels, not one threshold:**
  - **Show the output** when periodicity passes.
  - **Show it marked "irregular rhythm or artefact - check"** when only the beat scores pass.
  - **Withhold it** when neither passes.

  The irregular hearts, the people a clinician most wants to see, stay visible but flagged, and noise is
  never shown unflagged.
- The heart-failure recordings (`docs/15_patients.md`) include patients with frequent irregular beats;
  this check is not re-tuned on them.
